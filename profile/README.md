A free desktop app for pen-plotter art. Forty-eight tools sculpt 3D models, turn models, photographs and terrain into line drawings, and prepare every line for the plotter. Chain them into workflows of your own.

- **[AtlasToolkit](https://github.com/atlas-toolkit-org/AtlasToolkit)**: the app, for Windows, Linux and macOS. Free software, GPL-3.0-or-later.
- **[Preset packs](https://atlas-toolkit.org/presets/)**: tuned looks for every tool. The Basic pack is free.
- **[Early access](https://atlas-toolkit.org/early-access/)**: new releases before they are public, and every Complete pack.
